Platform Features

An overview of the monitoring parameters, algorithmic checks, and automatic controls integrated into the AgroGuard ecosystem.

Soil Moisture Monitoring

Sub-surface probes measure the Volumetric Water Content (VWC) of soil. Data is processed locally to avoid waterlogging and root rot, ensuring ideal root-zone respiration.

Ambient Temperature

Tracks atmospheric temperature levels to identify extreme heat waves or freezing frost events. Thermal data assists in calculating crop evaporation rates and stress levels.

Relative Humidity

Monitors moisture concentrations in the air. High humidity predictions are factored into disease vulnerability alerts, prompting preventive crop measures.

Rainfall Detection

A sensitive digital rain detector registers initial precipitation. Upon detection, all active automated irrigation schedules are immediately aborted to conserve water.

Water Tank Monitoring

Ultrasonic level sensors calculate available tank volume capacity. The system halts active water pumps if levels fall too low, preventing pump burnout.

Smart Irrigation Advisory

Fuses historical moisture datasets with live weather predictions. Dynamically schedules irrigation for cool evening hours to minimize evaporation losses.

Crop Recommendation Model

Recommends optimal crop options (e.g. Maize, Rice, or Pulses) matching current farm properties, helping farmers optimize yields and crop rotation schedules.

Farm Health Index

A unified agricultural index grading farm plot wellness on a 0-100 scale, calculated dynamically by combining moisture stability, ambient stress, and tank availability.

AgroGuard

A smart, low-cost agronomic decision support system providing telemetry automation and water conservation metrics to farming communities.
